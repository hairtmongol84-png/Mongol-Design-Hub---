Affiliate Agreement & Verification

To maintain the integrity of the Mongol Design Hub, all partners must adhere to the DOCUMENT_114 compliance standards, including verified communication channels.

article Document_114: Affiliate Terms

1. Scope of Promotion

Affiliates are strictly prohibited from utilizing unauthorized promotional materials. All marketing assets must be sourced from the Mongol Design Hub official portal. Failure to comply results in immediate partnership termination.

2. Communication Standards

All active affiliates are required to maintain a verified phone communication line. This ensures real-time compliance updates and secure transactional notifications between the Hub and the Partner.

warning

Critical: Marketing Ban

Any affiliate found engaging in spam, misleading "click-bait" advertisements, or unauthorized use of the Mongol Design Hub trademark will be permanently blacklisted with total forfeiture of accrued earnings.

3. Verification Procedures

The verification process involves a two-step authentication method. Partnerships are only "Active" once the OTP (One-Time Password) cycle is completed successfully through the registered mobile device.
