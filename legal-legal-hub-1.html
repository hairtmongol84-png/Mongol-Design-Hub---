Mongol Design Hub operates strictly as a neutral technology intermediary. We provide the digital infrastructure connecting verified designers with global merchants. We do not take ownership of physical goods, nor do we act as an agent, partner, or joint venturer for any user.

All transactions executed through this platform are direct bipartite agreements between the Buyer and the Seller. Platform owner liability is strictly limited to technology uptime and secure data transmission as defined in Section 4 of the Platform Use Agreement.
